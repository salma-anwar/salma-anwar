### 1. EpistemicBalance — Evaluating LLM Decision Stability

**AI Safety | LLM Evaluation | Prompt Engineering | Python**

*Collaborative Research — Apart Research AI x Epistemics Research Sprint, November 2026*

Co-developed an LLM evaluation framework investigating how misleading and corrective investigator suggestions influence model decisions during evidence-based reasoning.

**Highlights:**
- Evaluated 550 fictional criminal investigation cases from MuSR and NarraCrime.
- Benchmarked Qwen 3.7 Flash and GLM 5.3 Flash.
- Compared baseline reasoning with misleading and corrective investigator interventions.
- Measured answer-flipping rates, confidence changes, and decision stability.
- Identified a 17.6% harmful flip rate and a 96.0% corrective transition rate.
